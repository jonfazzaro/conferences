## Using the Tools Without Losing Your Soul: Agentic Coding for the Reluctant

by [Rachael McQuater](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rachaelmcquater/)

We're *sad*. But being sad about technology obsoleting the craft of artisans is not a new human experience.

If you don't participate, someone else (with less integrity) will. If you opt out of embracing AI, it just means the discourse has one less person with real skills and scruples.

Say goodbye to 

+ meaningless cognitive load
+ weird little bugs
+ babysitting 
+ context switching

Encode what you do into something someone else can see.

Don't write the prompt, ask the agent for the prompt.

Rethink the norms: we don't raise the individual plants, we tend the garden.

Splashing in the shallow end doesn't get us anywhere. Learning happens when you jump into the deep end.
